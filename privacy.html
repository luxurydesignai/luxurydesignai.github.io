Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Luxury Design AI ("we") operates a private, self-hosted scheduling tool that uploads videos to our own YouTube channel. The tool is used only by us and is not offered to the public.

Google user data

With our permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and read basic channel information (channel name and avatar) for our own account. OAuth tokens are stored only on our own computer. We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted.
